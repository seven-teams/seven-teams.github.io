<p style="text-align:center;">
Where's the meaning? How can I understand?<br>
Where's the Mother? Where's the love within?<br>
Frustration all over me! Can't see the life!<br>
<br>
Where is this going? What am I doing here?
<br>
Mother, please help!<br>
Help me to see the love<br>
Help me to feel the joy<br>
<br>
Give strength to this waning pulse<br>
Let my attention move within<br>
Let me find the place where you reside<br>
<br>
Mother!<br>
<br>
Open up this heart of stone<br>
Let it pump the cooling flow of joy<br>
<br>
Let it wash away<br>
this filthy mud of darkened thoughts<br>
<br>
Let me find myself! Let me lose myself!<br>
Let me give myself to you!<br>
<br>
Let not frustration cover up the beauty of my soul<br>
Lift me up so high above<br>
<br>
Let not the darkness swallow up the peace of mind<br>
Let the silence soothe my thoughts<br>
<br>
I want to be immersed<br>
in soulful meaning of the highest<br>
<br>
Want to spread<br>
the joyful meaning of the self<br>
<br>
Want to give<br>
the loving purpose of being one<br>
<br>
Make me a fountain<br>
Strong enough to spray afar<br>
the loving drops<br>
And quench the thirst of many<br>
</p>
