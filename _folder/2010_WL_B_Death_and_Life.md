<p style="text-align:center;">
Is death the end, or the beginning?<br>
Is death an occasion to be mourned or to be celebrated?<br>
Is death to be feared or to be welcomed?<br>
<br>
From nature's point of view<br>
death is simply part of life<br>
to make room for new generations<br>
to provide space and time for evolution<br>
<br>
For the eternal part of us<br>
death is simple a possibility<br>
for a rest and a change of cloths<br>
<br>
For our human mind, though, death is indeed an ending<br>
whether it's our own mind or the mind of someone else<br>
<br>
So, it's ok and sensible to grieve<br>
It's ok to show a little fear<br>
<br>
It makes a difference if we have time for anticipation<br>
or if we don't know when death comes knocking<br>
<br>
Preparation helps<br>
It also helps to have lived a life with purpose<br>
<br>
It helps if we don't live life towards our death<br>
but if we live our life with respect for life<br>
to develop and do good<br>
for ourselves and the life of others<br>
<br>
It doesn't help to run from death<br>
<br>
It's better to make a life worth living<br>
It's better to allow for time to prepare oneself<br>
if one knows that death could be approaching<br>
<br>
Often we spend so much time with extending life<br>
that we don't have much time enjoying it<br>
<br>
I would like to know that my life has made a difference<br>
That I have learned and have evolved<br>
That I have helped humanity evolve at least a little bit<br>
<br>
And at the end of life, welcome death<br>
like an old friend, with open arms<br>
And leave a little love behind<br>
for those who follow after me
</p>
