<p style="text-align:center;">
Recently
I was thinking: Where is my alliance?<br>
Is it with good, is it with evil?<br>
<br>
I know: It is not!<br>
<br>
I was thinking: Should I follow my heart?<br>
Or should I follow my head?<br>
<br>
I know: I should not!<br>
<br>
My alliance is with a vision<br>
that I had on an airport in Paris<br>
<br>
Five minutes before She stepped out - into my sight<br>
I felt the friendship of brothers and sisters<br>
united in their love for their Mother<br>
<br>
No groups, no races, no religions<br>
No leaders, no workers, no thinkers<br>
<br>
No healthy, no sick, no heroes, no failures<br>
Humanity united in their love for their Mother<br>
<br>
When she stepped out through that door<br>
I was surprised<br>
<br>
Surprised how small she was<br>
Surprised how vulnerable she seemed<br>
<br>
Her smile captured my eyes<br>
I gave her a flower<br>
She accepted my heart<br>
<br>
Seeing her smile<br>
lifted me beyond her human form<br>
All the way to God<br>
<br>
Seeing her children<br>
made me fall in love with humanity<br>
<br>
Humanity in all its weaknesses<br>
capable of such a love<br>
<br>
Such a love<br>
of children for their Mother<br>
<br>
Such a love<br>
of children for each other
</p>
