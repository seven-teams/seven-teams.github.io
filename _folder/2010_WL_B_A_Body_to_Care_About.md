<p style="text-align:center;">
Recently a young dog<br>
joined our family<br>
<br>
After some time with it<br>
I realized something about myself<br>
<br>
The relationship with my body could be similar<br>
to the relationship with my young dog<br>
<br>
I give it attention, I give it treats, I give it exercise<br>
<br>
Sometimes it needs training<br>
Sometimes it needs discipline<br>
<br>
It cannot always get what it likes<br>
neither can it always do what it wants<br>
<br>
Like for the young dog that I love<br>
I need to discriminate about what my body can get<br>
<br>
Force it a little to do what is good for it<br>
And take away the things that could do it harm<br>
<br>
It is the understanding of myself, which makes me human<br>
My body is just like that of the animals it did evolve from<br>
<br>
I realized<br>
that I should treat my body like an animal<br>
which I love and care about
</p>
