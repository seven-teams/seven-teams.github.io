<p style="text-align:center;">
Children of one Mother<br>
<br>
Listening to the wisdom of old<br>
We feel safe<br>
<br>
Playing with each other<br>
in her watchful gaze<br>
We feel safe<br>
<br>
Her feet in the corner of our eyes<br>
We feel safe<br>
<br>
Climbing on the mountain,<br>
aware of her presence<br>
We feel safe
<br>
When the stranger’s voice is whispering<br>
It may sound like fun<br>
<br>
The stranger’s gaze is tempting us<br>
May make us feel all grown up
<br>
The stranger’s tasks lead to new adventures<br>
May make us feel curious<br>
<br>
The voices of wisdom sound so stale<br>
When we start to grow up<br>
<br>
The children’s playful gestures seem so boring<br>
When we start to grow up<br>
<br>
The Mother’s protective gaze appears so limiting<br>
When we start to grow up<br>
In the strange world<br>
We don’t need our Mother<br>
In the strange world<br>
We are above the others<br>
In the strange world<br>
The stranger’s affection sets us apart<br>
<br>
The Mother feels so far<br>
When the confusion sets in<br>
<br>
All grown up<br>
The safety of her voice has lost its comfort<br>
<br>
And being lost in our greatness loses its fun<br>
When the guilt sets in<br>
<br>
Luckily the Mother’s loving hands are never far<br>
When we become small again
<br>
Luckily her gaze is still as loving<br>
When we move back into the presence of her being
<br>
It may take a little time<br>
But the play of the children will be sweet again
</p>
