<p style="text-align:center;">
The painter paints with Love<br>
a painter in the painting<br>
<br>
Behold, the painter’s Love<br>
makes the painter in the painting<br>
a source of life<br>
<br>
The painter looks with Joy<br>
at the painter in the painting<br>
<br>
The painter in the painting<br>
paints with Love<br>
other painters in the painting<br>
<br>
The painter in the painting looks with Joy<br>
at the other painters in the painting<br>
<br>
Behold, the painter in the painting’s Love<br>
makes the other painters in the painting<br>
full of life<br>
<br>
The other painters in the painting see<br>
their painter in the painting<br>
<br>
The other painters paint with Love<br>
an image<br>
of their painter in the painting<br>
<br>
The other painters look with Joy<br>
at the image<br>
of their painter in the painting<br>
<br>
The other painters look with Love<br>
at each other<br>
<br>
The painter looks with Joy and Love<br>
at the image<br>
of the painter in the painting<br>
<br>
The painter paints with love<br>
the next painter in the painting
</p>
