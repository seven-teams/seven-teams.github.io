<p style="text-align:center;">
Human Cultures<br>
are like flowers on a field<br>
Sprouting, budding with the rain of love<br>
<br>
There comes along the time of spring<br>
See the beauty, inhale the fragrance<br>
<br>
Like the bees, the humming birds<br>
drink the nectar of generosity<br>
<br>
Inspired like a painting<br>
filled with Mother Nature's art<br>
<br>
One step at a time it becomes complete<br>
The culture of the heart
</p>
