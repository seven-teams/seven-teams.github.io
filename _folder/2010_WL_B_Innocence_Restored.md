<p style="text-align:center;">
My innocence was hurt<br>
when I was but a child<br>
<br>
My innocence was hurt<br>
by others who were but children<br>
<br>
My attention was directed<br>
towards baser things<br>
<br>
My attention was corrupted <br>
ithout knowing how to value me myself<br>
<br>
When the Mother awakened in my being<br>
She started to heal the child in me<br>
<br>
She started to show me how to value me myself<br>
She started to show me how to respect me myself<br>
<br>
When the child within awakened, the joy was just intense<br>
More intense than any joy I might have felt before<br>
<br>
Much more intense, than any happiness<br>
gained from short time satisfaction<br>
<br>
Slowly the child in me grew stronger<br>
Not so easily affected by baser feelings<br>
Not so easily disturbed and scared<br>
<br>
The attention stronger, wisdom protecting me<br>
<br>
Chastity does not come from outward actions<br>
Chastity does not prevent expression to the one I wed<br>
<br>
Chastity allows me to respect me myself<br>
Chastity allows me to express my love with care<br>
<br>
Chastity has restored my innocence
</p>
