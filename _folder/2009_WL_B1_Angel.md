<br>

<p style="text-align:center;">
Angel<br>
<br>
How beautiful you are<br>
How wise and generous you are<br>
Your touch creates a spark of joy in me<br>
<br>
Angel<br>
<br>
You transform the world around<br>
Slowly you generate, slowly you dissolve<br>
Slowly you create, slowly you resolve<br>
<br>
Angel<br>
<br>
How beautiful you are<br>
How much joy you give to me
</p>
