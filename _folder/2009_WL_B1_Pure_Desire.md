<p style="text-align:center;">
Deep inside my being<br>
my soul is permeated with pure desire<br>
<br>
Desire that is steady<br>
Desire that is full of hope<br>
Desire generated by light within<br>
<br>
As this pure desire<br>
seeps through to the surface<br>
<br>
It encounters areas of darkness<br>
It encounters muddy swamps of fear<br>
It encounters hardened stones of rejection<br>
It encounters forces of past misdeeds<br>
<br>
So, when it reaches the surface of my being<br>
Sometimes, it has become a darkened stream<br>
<br>
Mostly, though, it is just colored<br>
Enriched with experience and knowledge<br>
Still carrying the light that gave it birth within<br>
<br>
Sometimes, though,<br>
When this desire, still pure, touches my heart<br>
the enlightened flow shoots through my being like a fountain<br>
<br>
The fountain then creates a river<br>
A river streaming with love and light<br>
Desires putting my inner turbines to work<br>
<br>
Creating actions<br>
<br>
Actions, gentle and benevolent<br>
Actions, which make the Mother smile<br>
Actions, flowing with love, compassion, evolution<br>
Actions, which strive for union with the greater one<br>
<br>
Actions, which are helping pure desires<br>
enriched with love and light<br>
streaming to the surface<br>
in my fellow human beings
</p>
