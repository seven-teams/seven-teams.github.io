<p style="text-align:center;">
Innocence in me&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;
Like a tree in the fall&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;
Leaves return in spring&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;
&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;Creativity
&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;Inspired by knowledge
&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;Bliss reaches the sun
Master of my peace&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;
No Illusion leads me&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;
The sea is my home&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;
&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;Mother is safety
&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;Heart is home of my spirit
&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;Eternal I am
Diplomat of joy&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;
Dancing in the universe&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;
Evolving myself&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;
&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;Forgiving myself
&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;Forgiving mind and ego
&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;Becoming the light
Love is everlasting
Everything reflects in my soul
Children of Mother<br>
</p>
