<p style="text-align:center;">
My love for humanity is real<br>
My love is not temporary<br>
My love is unconditional<br>
<br>
Love requires a connection<br>
Love requires a heart full of truth<br>
Love requires knowledge and understanding<br>
<br>
My love is not always obvious to everyone<br>
My love is not always projected onto an individual<br>
My love is not always expressed in pleasantries<br>
<br>
Love flows to where it’s needed<br>
Love penetrates darkness until it reaches light<br>
Love acts slowly to dissolve the obstacles to what is good<br>
<br>
My love seeks to understand the variety of human nature<br>
My love reaches out to those who can make a difference<br>
My love enjoys the happiness of others when it’s real<br>
<br>
Love makes the flow of love grow stronger<br>
Love hollows out my being to purify the flow of love<br>
My love for humanity originates from the source of love itself<br>
<br>
Love is the answer for the future of humanity
</p>
