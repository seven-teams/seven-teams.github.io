<p style="text-align:center;">
Skin color, cultural heritage, religious background<br>
might all influence behavior – but none of them define it<br>
<br>
The inner workings of our mind are<br>
much more subject to individual personality<br>
<br>
Initial wiring of our brain<br>
intelligence, sexual predisposition<br>
might be part of the making<br>
of our own, individual personality<br>
But these do not define us permanently either<br>
<br>
We are subject to shaping by our peers and parents<br>
We are always subject to learning and development<br>
Small realizations give us joy and change us every day<br>
<br>
Children can learn and adapt much quicker<br>
<br>
But even adults change their mind<br>
and are changed by other’s minds as well<br>
The child in us always wants to learn and grow<br>
Wants to understand, to be loved and understood<br>
<br>
So let’s love each other, teach each other, help each other<br>
So that we can learn and understand<br>
change and grow each day<br>
<br>
Never to be imprisoned<br>
by the walls of a never changing personality
</p>
