<p style="text-align:center;">
Love of the Spirit<br>
<br>
Love – from a mother to her child<br>
Love – from a brother to his sister<br>
<br>
Love – pure and beautiful<br>
<br>
Love – unchanging and unconditional<br>
Love – provided by the mother within<br>
<br>
Love – enhancing the joy from inside<br>
Love – planting the seed for a spiritual life<br>
<br>
Love of the Spirit
</p>
