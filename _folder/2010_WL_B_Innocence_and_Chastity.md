<p style="text-align:center;">
Innocence is not naivety<br>
Chastity is not abstinence<br>
<br>
Naivety does not give joy<br>
Abstinence does not preclude desire<br>
<br>
The joy of innocence is like looking at a baby<br>
<br>
Normal people just feel joy<br>
and the longing to show their love<br>
<br>
No desires, no fear, no expectations<br>
Just the peaceful joy of feeling love<br>
<br>
The joy of chastity is only felt when the love is pure<br>
<br>
The same joy might be briefly glimpsed<br>
when two lovers do unite<br>
<br>
But this glimpse of joy is short and fleeting<br>
A one thousand part of the joy of truly feeling chaste<br>
<br>
Chastity has no connection to physical behavior<br>
<br>
It cannot be reached by abstinence<br>
It cannot be gained by blocking out the senses<br>
It is the joy of being pure; it is the joy of being true<br>
This joy is felt when the child within is happy<br>
<br>
This joy is felt when looking at oneself<br>
is like looking at a baby child<br>
<br>
To feel the love, to feel the peace<br>
To feel the joy of being pure and true
</p>
