<p style="text-align:center;">
I think of the future<br>
Is there sorrow<br>
I think?<br>
<br>
I think of the past<br>
Is there sorrow<br>
I think?<br>
<br>
My mind goes to you<br>
My heart fills with joy!<br>
<br>
I might try to think<br>
of the sorrow<br>
the future might hold<br>
<br>
I might try to think<br>
of the sorrow<br>
the past experiences have told<br>
<br>
But then<br>
I feel the presence of you<br>
in my heart<br>
<br>
I feel the Mother<br>
playfully made us meet<br>
Divine is her art<br>
<br>
She looks out to the future<br>
with a smile<br>
While we worry about our part<br>
<br>
What sorrow can there be?<br>
What worry can there be?<br>
With your image<br>
dancing next to my spirit’s light<br>
<br>
How doest the future<br>
look anything but bright<br>
with our Mother’s loving eyes<br>
set upon our lives?<br>
<br>
She planted the seed<br>
Embedded in the melting of our lives<br>
our fate became one by her vision divine<br>
<br>
By faith in her a tree will rise<br>
Spreading its fruits sublime
</p>
