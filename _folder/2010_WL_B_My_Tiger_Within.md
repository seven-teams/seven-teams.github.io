<p style="text-align:center;">
The tiger within me watches<br>
Majestic, quiet, full of poise and confidence<br>
<br>
The tiger within me sits and waits alertly<br>
<br>
For the moment it should roar<br>
For the moment it should battle<br>
For the moment it should protect<br>
<br>
For the moment it should struck fear<br>
into the hearts of those who hurt the meek<br>
<br>

The tiger within me sits and waits alertly<br>
<br>
For the moment it will jump<br>
and ravage the minds of those<br>
who preach hate and desperation<br>
<br>
The tiger with me sits and waits alertly<br>
<br>
For the moment it is allowed to express its wrath<br>
Its wrath with the ones who take advantage of the innocent<br>
Its wrath with those who feel no shame<br>
at exploiting the unfortunate<br>
<br>
The tiger within me is not tame<br>
The tiger within me is not boisterous either<br>
It just quietly sits and waits its turn<br>
To express itself, once the time is right
</p>
