<p style="text-align:center;">
My house<br>
is still in progress,<br>
Waiting to be built, brick by brick<br>
<br>
The Mother helped me build a roof<br>
so the rain does not dissolve the mortar<br>
She gave me shelter in her house<br>
while the winter storms were blowing<br>
<br>
She showed me knowledge<br>
from which my house is built<br>
She led me to experiences<br>
which became the pillars<br>
She gave me love<br>
which holds the house together<br>
<br>
It’s so warm and cozy in the Mother’s house<br>
The light falls through the windows<br>
Revealing all the beauty<br>
Tables full of food and drinks<br>
To nurture me and give me strength<br>
<br>
Though the Mother<br>
really wants me to complete my house<br>
For others to find shelter<br>
For others to gain strength<br>
<br>
She wants me to have a place to share the knowledge<br>
She wants me to have a place to share the love<br>
<br>
So more often now she sends me out<br>
to continue working on my house<br>
Even if it’s cold, even if it’s windy<br>
<br>
She sends me out<br>
to collect experiences on my own<br>
Even if they are hard to carry, even if they hurt<br>
<br>
Her love, though<br>
is always there for me to take<br>
<br>
Which gives me hope<br>
my house will once become a palace<br>
Worthy of the Mother’s presence<br>
Worthy of the Mother’s love
</p>
