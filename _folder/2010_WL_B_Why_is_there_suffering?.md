<p style="text-align:center;">
If there is a God, then why then is there suffering?<br>
A question often asked and rarely understood<br>
<br>
No Pain, no gain<br>
is not just a phrase<br>
It is a reality of evolution<br>
<br>
We need to suffer<br>
so that we have motivation to grow<br>
so that we have motivation to change, to develop and evolve<br>
<br>
But the point of suffering is not the suffering<br>
The point is to find ways to alleviate the suffering<br>
<br>
The point is to use love and understanding<br>
To use compassion and creativity<br>
To use our hearts and minds<br>
to bring us closer<br>
to the goal of unity<br>
<br>
Together we are stronger<br>
Together we can give meaning to suffering<br>
by finding many ways to remove and overcome it<br>
<br>
Not only for ourselves but for the future of humanity
</p>
