<p style="text-align:center;">
There is a light<br>
in the beginning of life<br>
Born from the innocence of a child<br>
Born from the love of a mother<br>
<br>
A light in the youth<br>
Born from the urge to discover the world<br>
Born from the urge to rattle the walls of the world<br>
<br>
A light in the fullness of life<br>
Born from the songs of the birds and the bees<br>
Born from the laughter of children<br>
Born from the joy of enlightenment<br>
<br>
A light beyond the life<br>
Born when the soul touches<br>
the feet of the everlasting Mother<br>
This light of love, slowly and carringly<br>
permeates, displaces and dissolves<br>
the darkness of the world<br>
</p>
