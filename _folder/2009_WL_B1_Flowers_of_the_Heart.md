<p style="text-align:center;">
A flower of love<br>
grew in heart's wide and open land<br>
A flower of joy blossomed<br>
Spreading its fragrance far around<br>
<br>
They both joined in a most mysterious way<br>
Giving rise to nature’s most nourishing play<br>
<br>
The flowers became fruits<br>
And the fruits bore seeds<br>
The seeds grew flowers anew<br>
Spreading in heart’s wide and open land<br>
<br>
The Mother smiled watching the play<br>
It was her, who first planted the seeds<br>
It was out of her, the flowers first grew<br>
<br>
Giving rise to love and joy<br>
in heart’s wide and open land
</p>
